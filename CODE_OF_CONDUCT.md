# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

PicoTracker contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and users are expected to keep project spaces professional, constructive, and focused on the work.

Examples of acceptable behaviour include:

- giving technical feedback on the change rather than the person;
- disagreeing with evidence and clear reasoning;
- respecting different levels of experience;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- keeping reviews proportionate to the scope and risk of a contribution.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or targeted personal attacks;
- discriminatory or demeaning language;
- deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith participation;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sexualized conduct or other behaviour inappropriate for a professional technical project.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions around the project, and other spaces where someone is representing PicoTracker or acting in an official project role.

Technical criticism is welcome. Enforcement is about conduct, not disagreement.

## Reporting and enforcement

Public moderation concerns can be raised with the repository maintainer. If a conduct concern cannot safely be reported publicly, contact `@sylvesterkaczmarek` using the private contact method published on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

For security vulnerabilities rather than conduct concerns, use the private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or block a participant when necessary to protect the project and its contributors. Decisions should be based on the behaviour and context, applied consistently, and kept as private as practical when a report involves sensitive information.
